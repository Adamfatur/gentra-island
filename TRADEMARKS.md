# Name and logo

The code of Gentra Island is free software under the GPL-3.0-or-later. The name **Gentra Island** and the Gentra Island logo are not part of that license.

You may:

- Say that your project is based on, or works with, Gentra Island.
- Use the name and logo to refer to the official project, for example in an article or a review.

Please don't:

- Publish a fork or a modified build under the name Gentra Island or with its logo.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests the official project made or endorses your version.

If you ship a fork, give it its own name and icon, and keep the copyright and license notices as the GPL requires.

Questions: open an issue on the official repository.
