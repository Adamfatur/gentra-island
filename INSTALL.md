# Install Gentra Island

Download the file for your computer from the [Releases page](../../releases/latest), then follow the steps below. No terminal needed.

## Mac

Works on Intel Macs and Apple silicon (M1 and newer), macOS 11 or later.

1. Download the file ending in `.dmg`.
2. Open it and drag **Gentra Island** into **Applications**.
3. Open Gentra Island from Applications. The island appears at the top of your screen.

If the Mac says it can't check the app for malware, the app isn't signed yet. Open **System Settings**, go to **Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway**. You only need to do this once.

## Windows

Works on Windows 10 and 11.

1. Download the file ending in `-setup.exe`.
2. Double-click it and click **Install**. No admin password needed.
3. The island appears at the top of your screen.

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. This happens because the app isn't signed yet.

## Linux

Pick one:

- **Any distro**: download the `.AppImage`, right-click it, open **Properties**, turn on **Allow executing as program**, then double-click it.
- **Ubuntu, Debian, Mint, Pop!_OS**: download the `.deb` and double-click it to open it in your software center.
- **Fedora, openSUSE**: download the `.rpm` and double-click it.

## After installing

- Hover over the island to open it, or press `⌥⌘G` (Mac) or `Ctrl+Alt+G` (Windows and Linux).
- A short guide shows up the first time. You can replay it from Settings.
- To start Gentra Island when you log in, turn it on in the guide or in Settings.

## Uninstall

- **Mac**: quit from the menu bar icon, then drag Gentra Island from Applications to the Trash.
- **Windows**: Settings, Apps, find Gentra Island, Uninstall.
- **Linux**: delete the AppImage, or remove the package from your software center.
