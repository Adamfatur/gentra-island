# Code of Conduct

Gentra Island is made by people from many countries, in many languages. Everyone who takes part in its issues, pull requests, discussions, and translations should be able to do that without being harassed or put down.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new, or writing in a language that isn't their first.
- Disagree about the work, not about the person. Say what you think is wrong and why.
- Accept a decision you don't agree with once it's made, and move on.
- Give credit where it's due, including to the people whose work you build on.

## What isn't welcome

- Insults, slurs, or personal attacks, in any language.
- Harassment: following someone around the project, repeated unwanted contact, or threats.
- Sexual language or images, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, or using the project to advertise.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior bothers you, use **Report content** on the comment, issue, or pull request (the `...` menu) and choose to report it to the repository's maintainers. Only the maintainer sees the report. If it's about the maintainer, choose to report it to GitHub instead.

Reports are read within 7 days and kept private.

## What happens

Depending on what happened, the maintainer may hide or delete the content, lock a conversation, give a warning, or block someone from the repository, for a while or for good. The person reported gets a short explanation, unless that would put the reporter at risk.

This applies everywhere the project runs: this repository, its releases, and its website.
